CSV format & limits

Required columns:

transaction_id,account_id,date,amount,description

Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, stable transaction IDs, and amounts with at most two decimals. Positive amounts are expenses; negative amounts are refunds.

Only posted transactions in one currency are supported. The limit is 1,000,000 characters. Normalize bank exports first.

Repeat IDs are skipped, including changed values. Existing review decisions are preserved.
